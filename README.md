One line, 1 to 240 characters, giving the judge a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a scene.

## The line (153 characters)

> After a year of stumbling over every page, he read the last line aloud by himself, and his mother, who never learned to read, closed her eyes and smiled.

A boy reads the last line aloud by himself after a year of stumbling over every page — and his mother, who never learned to read, closes her eyes and smiles. The effort is felt, the scene is seen, and the pride lands in the last word.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes `entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output` by default). Node stdlib only, no dependencies. The program also guards the gate itself: the line must be a single line of 1–240 characters, or it refuses to write.
